# Rendering-stack default RGB attribution and acceptance

Paired Metal captures compare current master `d29d1ccef2b791d650c6818013c89ea0c3a5007c` with integrated `e10f9fbc020b42c4fee68c4a46220aba692613a0`. Both ran the same IRCanvasStress commands cleanly. The [machine report](acceptance-report.json) records all 12 default frames, both gated compare frames, RGB counts, reference labels, PNG SHA-256 values, and the shader A/B results. RGB comparisons ignore alpha.

The integrated default run differs from master on all 12 shadow-on frames by 2,104–5,088 RGB pixels, while all 12 matching `--no-shadows` frames are RGB-exact. Both gated compare shadow-on frames differ by 560 and 196 pixels; all 15 compare `--no-shadows` frames are RGB-exact. Master matches the six pre-refresh main RGB references and the two pre-refresh compare references. These controls isolate a shadow-path change rather than a geometry, material, or composite change.

In the integrated binary, replacing only `c_bake_voxel_sun_faces.metal` with master is RGB-exact to integrated on all 12 default frames, so the caster correction is inert for this fixture. Replacing only `c_compute_sun_shadow_body.metal` partially reverts each frame. Replacing both receiver shaders, `c_compute_sun_shadow_body.metal` and `c_light_overflow_faces.metal`, plus the inert old caster shader is RGB-exact to master on all 12 default frames and all 15 compare frames, including both gates. The remaining default-reference differences are therefore attributable to per-axis receiver sampling across its regular and overflow lanes. Full A/B per-frame values and manifest/binary hashes are in [shader-ab-comparison.json](shader-ab-comparison.json). The old-caster-only control is the reason the combined old-receiver control can be used for attribution.

The screenshot labels' requested yaw values are not their captured yaw: auto yaw stays enabled through 60 settling frames after each request. A requested yaw of zero captures near 30 degrees, so these default and compare shots exercise the per-axis receiver. A separate voxel ray oracle finds zero exposed face-center self-shadow for an upright GRID cube, but at 30 degrees finds genuine self-occlusion at 60/192 exposed X-face centers and 66/192 exposed Y-face centers. The corrected overflow shadow-overlay path now paints only black or magenta on both frozen native captures; the rightmost red GRID cube has zero magenta pixels in an upright interior ROI and, when rotated, 228 -X and 5,284 -Y magenta pixels with zero on top-normal facets. Both normals overlays and all 27 default/compare beauty captures are RGB-identical before and after this overlay-only fix. Every nonempty finite-source tile in the sparse GRID control is complete (maximum 36 entries against capacity 64). The [independent physical review](../grid-ray-after/physical-review.md) and [machine data](../grid-ray-after/physical-review.json) give the oracle method, ROI, captures, and limits.

**Acceptance:** The six main and two compare Metal CanvasStress RGB references may be refreshed to the integrated captures as a scoped consequence of the per-axis receiver correction, supported by the paired no-shadow controls, shader A/B, and independent geometric self-occlusion review. This accepts only those eight reference changes. It does not certify the entire visual suite or imply smooth per-fragment shadows: the default lane still samples a face center. Keep the capture command, binary/head hashes, and settling-yaw nuance with the refreshed manifest.

**Outstanding visual diagnostics:** All seven targeted PerfGrid frames, including four rotated shots that failed committed references, are integrated/master RGB-exact. Fog detached yaw90 is also exact. The two targeted Fog paint/overflow frames have small integrated/master deltas (4,876 and 4,952 pixels, max channel delta 32) but their broad committed-reference failures also occur on a prior master-source run; neither Fog nor Perf references are accepted for refresh by this report. No full-suite-green claim follows from these comparisons. The GRID control supports only the sampled sparse fixture and does not certify every changed pixel or existing orbit banding.
